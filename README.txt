MARAm's BIRTHDAY WEBSITE — VERSION 3 COMPLETE

LOCAL PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the birthday_site folder.
3. Double-click START_BIRTHDAY_WEBSITE.bat, or open index.html in a browser.
4. Keep the assets folder beside index.html.

PUBLISH ONLINE (FREE OPTION: NETLIFY DROP)
1. Extract this ZIP on your computer.
2. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop in your browser.
3. Drag the entire birthday_site folder (the folder containing index.html and assets) onto the page.
4. Netlify will publish it and give you a public website link.
5. Open that link on your phone to test the photos, games, letters, and music/video.

NOTES
- The YouTube video/music is started by the opening button and stays in the background while navigating between pages. Browsers may require the opening click before playback.
- The YouTube player can show an error when opening the site directly as a local file; test it after publishing online.
- Keep index.html and the assets folder together when uploading.
